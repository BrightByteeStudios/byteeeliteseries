OUR HERITAGE

Since 1998, LUMIÈRE BOTANICALS has been a quiet revolution in luxury skincare. We source only from small, sustainable farms and work with scientists who share our reverence for the living world. Every formula is a meditation — patient, precise, and deeply considered. We believe radiance is not manufactured. It is uncovered.
